# Vift — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-24_

Vift ("the app", "we") is a commute timing app for iOS that shows when to leave for departures from stops you configure. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. Vift is operated by **Richard Magnusson**, based in Sweden.

The short version: Vift has no user accounts, collects no personal information, and keeps your routes on your device. The only data sent off your device is anonymous usage statistics and the transit lookups needed to show departures.

## Data stored on your device

Your route configuration — the stops, lines, walk times, and schedules you set up — is stored locally on your device and shared only between the app and its widget. It is **not** transmitted to us; we operate no server that receives it. Deleting the app removes this data.

## Transit data (Trafiklab)

To show departures, the app sends the stop identifier for your configured routes to the **Trafiklab Realtime API** and receives departure times in return. These requests are necessary for the app to function. Trafiklab is an independent third party and processes these requests under its own terms. Transit data is provided by Trafiklab (Data from Trafiklab.se) under a CC‑BY license.

## Analytics (TelemetryDeck)

To understand whether the app works for people during this beta — for example, how many people who install it go on to set up a route — Vift uses **TelemetryDeck**, a privacy‑focused analytics service.

- The events collected are limited to anonymous usage signals such as app launches, creating your first route, and whether a widget is active.
- TelemetryDeck is configured in its default anonymized mode. It does **not** receive your name, email, precise location, advertising identifiers, or the contents of your routes.
- Identifiers used to group events are anonymized (hashed) so that we cannot use them to identify you or single you out.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites, and Vift contains no advertising.

## Location

Vift does **not** collect or use your device location. Routes are activated by the time‑of‑day schedules you configure, not by where you are.

## Beta testing via TestFlight

While Vift is distributed as a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects information from testers — such as your name, email address, app usage, and crash logs — and shares some of it with us as the developer, in accordance with Apple's [TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/) and privacy policy. We use any such information only to improve the app.

## Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA: our legal basis for the anonymous analytics described above is our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app. Because this data is anonymized and Vift holds no account or personal identifiers, we are generally unable to identify an individual or link data back to a specific person, which limits the practical scope of individual data requests. If you have any questions about your data or this policy, contact us and we will help where we can.

## Data retention and location of processing

We retain only aggregate, anonymized analytics. Transit lookups are not stored by us. Service providers may process data outside Sweden: TelemetryDeck and Trafiklab operate within the EU; Apple/TestFlight may process tester data in the United States under its own safeguards.

## Children

Vift is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
