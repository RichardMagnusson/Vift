# Vift — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-12_

Vift ("the app", "we") is a commute timing app for iOS that shows when to leave for departures from stops you configure. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. Vift is operated by **Richard Magnusson**, based in Sweden.

The short version: Vift has no user accounts, collects no personal information, and keeps your routes and your location on your device. The only data sent off your device is anonymous usage statistics and the transit lookups needed to show departures.

## Data stored on your device

Your route configuration — the stops, lines, walk times, and schedules you set up, along with the coordinates of your stops — is stored locally on your device and shared only between the app and its widget. If you allow location access, your most recent approximate position is stored the same way (see Location). This data is **not** transmitted to us; we operate no server that receives it. Deleting the app removes this data.

## Transit data (Trafiklab)

To show departures, the app sends the stop identifier for your configured routes, and the text you type when searching for a stop, to **Trafiklab's APIs** (Realtime and ResRobot) and receives departure and stop information in return. Your location is never included in these requests. These requests are necessary for the app to function. Trafiklab is an independent third party and processes these requests under its own terms. Transit data is provided by Trafiklab (Data from Trafiklab.se) under a CC‑BY license.

## Analytics (TelemetryDeck)

To understand whether the app works for people during this beta — for example, how many people who install it go on to set up a route — Vift uses **TelemetryDeck**, a privacy‑focused analytics service.

- The events collected are limited to anonymous usage signals: app launches, creating your first route, whether a widget is placed and in which sizes, taps on the widget's route switcher, and simple counts about your setup and how the widget performs — for example how many routes you have, whether departures loaded or were shown from cache, and whether "Nearest stop first" is on and location access is granted (never the location itself).
- With each event, TelemetryDeck records basic technical information such as app version, iOS version, device model, and language, region and time-zone settings.
- TelemetryDeck is configured in its default anonymized mode. It does **not** receive your name, email, any location, advertising identifiers, or the contents of your routes.
- Identifiers used to group events are anonymized (hashed) so that we cannot use them to identify you or single you out.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites, and Vift contains no advertising.

## Location

If you allow it, Vift uses your device's approximate location to show the route whose stop is closest to you first when several routes are active at the same time. Your location is used only on your device. The most recent approximate position is kept on the device (and in its backups) and is **never sent** to us, Trafiklab, TelemetryDeck or anyone else. Routes are still activated by the schedules you configure, not by where you are. You can turn this off with "Nearest stop first" in the app, or deny location access in iOS Settings; Vift then uses your own route order.

## Beta testing via TestFlight

While Vift is distributed as a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects information from testers — such as your name, email address, app usage, and crash logs — and shares some of it with us as the developer, in accordance with Apple's [TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/) and privacy policy. We use any such information only to improve the app.

## Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA: our legal basis for the anonymous analytics described above is our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app. Because this data is anonymized and Vift holds no account or personal identifiers, we are generally unable to identify an individual or link data back to a specific person, which limits the practical scope of individual data requests. If you have any questions about your data or this policy, contact us and we will help where we can.

## Data retention and location of processing

We retain only aggregate, anonymized analytics. Transit lookups are not stored by us. Service providers may process data outside Sweden: TelemetryDeck and Trafiklab operate within the EU; Apple/TestFlight may process tester data in the United States under its own safeguards.

## Children

Vift is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
